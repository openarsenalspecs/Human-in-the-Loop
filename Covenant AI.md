# Covenant AI

### Intelligence That Answers to You.

Covenant AI is an open-source, AGPL 3.0+ licensed artificial intelligence infrastructure platform focused on human authority, bounded autonomy, transparency, verifiable execution, privacy, and accountable AI systems.

Covenant AI is designed as a complete alternative to traditional AI agent infrastructures by ensuring that artificial intelligence systems operate only within explicitly authorized boundaries defined by the user.

The project is built around a simple principle:

> Artificial intelligence should never possess assumed authority.

Every action must be:
- Authorized
- Bounded
- Traceable
- Explainable
- Revocable
- Accountable

---

# Vision

Modern AI systems increasingly act autonomously across applications, devices, and networks. Most current architectures prioritize capability and interoperability, but lack enforceable guarantees for human control, transparency, and authority limitations.

Covenant AI exists to establish a human-authority-first model for artificial intelligence where:
- users maintain continuous control,
- permissions are temporary and scoped,
- actions are verifiable,
- and AI systems remain subordinate to the humans they serve.

---

# Core Principles

## Human Authority First
AI systems may only operate under explicit user authorization.

## Bounded Autonomy
Every action, task, and workflow is constrained by enforceable limits.

## Transparency by Design
All decisions and actions are explainable and auditable.

## Revocable Permissions
Authority can be withdrawn instantly at any time.

## Privacy as Infrastructure
User data is never treated as ambient or unrestricted.

## Open Governance
Standards, development, and security reviews remain transparent and community-driven.

# ProofShield

## The Shield Against Document Fraud

ProofShield is an AGPL-3.0+ licensed, modular document authenticity, provenance, and fraud prevention system designed to protect digital and physical documents from unauthorized alteration, forgery, and AI-generated manipulation.

ProofShield creates a certified source of truth through Certified Mirror Images, cryptographic document fingerprints, provenance records, privacy-preserving verification, and hardware-backed security. The system enables any device, camera, scanner, or software platform to verify whether a document is authentic, altered, or unverifiable.

ProofShield is designed as an open verification infrastructure for the future of digital trust, providing organizations and individuals with the ability to identify document manipulation and preserve authenticity in an era of advanced image generation technologies.

## Core Objectives

- Establish a trusted original document reference.
- Provide a Certified Authentic Seal for verified documents.
- Detect and visibly identify unauthorized alterations.
- Detect AI-generated document manipulation.
- Provide privacy-preserving verification.
- Support universal device and software compatibility.
- Enable open integration through APIs and SDKs.
- Provide hardware-backed security options.
- Create a transparent document provenance ecosystem.

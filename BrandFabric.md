# BrandFabric

## The Governance Layer for Enterprise Creative AI

BrandFabric is an open-source AI governance platform designed to help organizations control, manage, and align generative AI workflows with their brand identity, creative standards, compliance requirements, and organizational values.

BrandFabric provides an intelligent identity layer between organizations and AI systems, enabling businesses to create AI-generated content while preserving consistency, ownership, transparency, and control.

Rather than replacing creative professionals, BrandFabric empowers teams by giving AI a deeper understanding of an organization's identity, products, messaging, and creative rules.

---

# Overview

Generative AI has made content creation faster, but organizations face new challenges:

- Maintaining brand consistency across AI-generated content
- Preventing unauthorized changes to products and messaging
- Managing AI-generated asset ownership
- Enforcing creative and compliance standards
- Maintaining transparency around AI-assisted workflows
- Avoiding vendor lock-in with proprietary AI systems

BrandFabric addresses these challenges by providing an open-source governance layer that connects:

- Brand identity systems
- Creative workflows
- AI models
- Digital assets
- Compliance policies
- Human approval processes

BrandFabric enables organizations to build AI systems that understand not only what to create, but how the organization should be represented.
